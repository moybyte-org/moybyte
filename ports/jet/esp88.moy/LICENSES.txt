The film's car, city and artwork are original procedural work of that
example: its code builds the geometry, and its tools/prepare_assets.py
and tools/prepare_credits.py generated the textures and the closing
credits. assets.bin holds that artwork as this cart reads it: the
textures as the example ships them, the glow and the credits scaled
to two thirds, the console's frame against the film's.
