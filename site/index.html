The idea

A computer that's meant to be taken apart.

Everything on a Moybyte — the games, the drawings, the little tools — can be opened up and changed. Playing with something and remaking it are the same thing here.

Everything opens up

Every game, tool, and even the home screen can be looked inside and changed. Nothing is sealed shut.

Room to make things

No feeds, no autoplay, no endless scroll — just space to make something and try it out.

Change it and see it

Adjust something, press Run, and it happens right away. Small changes, quick to try.

Offline and private

Works without the internet. No accounts, no sign-ups, no chat. What a child makes stays theirs.

Grows with your kid

It grows as they do.

The same little computer suits a five-year-old and a twelve-year-old — they just go as deep as they want. There's no "now learn to code" moment.
