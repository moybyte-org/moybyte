The idea

Most kids' screens are sealed shut. This one isn't.

On the usual tablet, the good stuff is locked behind glass. Here a kid can open the game they're playing and change how it works.

Even the home screen

Not just the games — a kid can open the launcher, the menus, the console itself, and change how it works.

Room to make things

No feeds, no autoplay, no endless scroll — just space to make something and try it out.

Safe to experiment

Try a change, run it, undo it. Nothing breaks — so a kid keeps poking at how things work.

Offline and private

Works without the internet. No accounts, no sign-ups, no chat. What a child makes stays theirs.

For ages 5–12

It grows as they do.

One console for a five-year-old and a twelve-year-old. Tapping leads to blocks, blocks lead to real code — no sudden "now learn to code" moment.
