Learn to make, not just to use

A computer a kid can take apart from the inside.

Most kids' tech teaches you to operate a screen. Moybyte hands over a whole little workshop: open any cartridge — a game, the wallpaper, a widget, even a tool — read its code, tweak it, and make it yours.

Everything is a cartridge

Games, wallpapers, widgets, lessons, tools — one editable bundle format. Open any of them.

Make, don't consume

No passive media, no open internet in kid mode. Creation is the whole point.

Instant edit → run

Change one line, press Run, see the computer change. The loop that hooks makers.

Local-first & parent-safe

Offline by default. No accounts, no open chat, no child real-name. Sharing stays local.

One console, three bodies

The Moybyte family.

Every tier runs the same console and the same cartridges — a 320×240 world in the MOY64 palette. What a child makes on one runs on all of them; only the body differs.
