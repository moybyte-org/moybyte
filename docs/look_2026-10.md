# The 2026-10 look: the build plan (2026-10-15)

The target is `docs/media/look_2026-10/`: `DESIGN.md` is the look, `GAPS.md`
what the engine lacks and what each gap costs, `index.txt` names every mockup
and `mockups.py` draws them. This doc orders that work into steps and copies
neither; where they disagree, `DESIGN.md` wins. The look replaces D1 of
`docs/theming_2026-09.md` §1; the home is `docs/shell_layout_2026-10.md` §12's
component, restyled. Numbers live in **#66** (frames, the T-Deck and S3
boards) and **#58** (the P4 boards).

## Decisions (2026-10-15)

- **Taste is the orchestrator's** (owner); engine and system changes are
  welcome for the look.
- **Theme colours are RGB** (GAPS 1). This reverses
  `docs/theming_2026-09.md` §11's decline, which is corrected there. A
  cart's colour stays `& 63`.
- **The seed set is four:** Moy (dark and light; the default, replacing
  `night`), Paper (light only), Studio (dark only) and Contrast (dark and
  light). Every other seed is deleted. Under the no-migrations rule, a stored
  theme name that no longer exists reads as absent and resolves to the
  default.
- **Deferred:** a manifest description and genre, and a best-score verb
  (both moy-spec's); the details pane shows what GAPS 9 lists without them.
- **DESIGN's role names are the mockups' vocabulary, not new roles.** Step 2
  maps `bg`, `text`, `line` and `dim` onto roles that already exist, and adds
  only what has none. The moonlit top is `lit`, because `edge` is already a
  role.
- **The desk home is the carousel arrangement laid out on wide glass**, not
  a fourth `home` value (L0, L14).
- **A new look is `moy`'s theme data** (fonts, frame style, surfaces,
  metrics), so old seeds keep their pixels until step 23. A structural step
  (the marks, a screen's rows) re-baselines every config once, by name.

## How every step runs

Each step is one worktree (`tools/worktree.py new NAME`) from `dev`'s tip,
landed by cherry-pick as one commit that marks the step "Landed
YYYY-MM-DD" here, which the review kit reads. A parallel step re-bakes
`tests/shell_goldens/hashes.json` after its rebase, never merging it by hand.
One board agent runs at a time.

**Gates, in order:** `make test` with the step's net,
`tools/preflight.sh --web`, `tools/board.py pass tdeck p4`, the perf check,
the review.

**Perf** is `tools/p4_bench.py` on the named boards, beside the same
scenario on `dev`'s tip, recorded in #66 or #58. 2 ms or more is signal; 3 ms
or more needs an explanation in the commit; every idle scenario paints zero;
the P4 desk is bandwidth-bound, so a full-window pass is measured there.
Every build fails under its board's `headroom_floor_kib` (board.toml).

**Review.** A reviewer with no context gets only the step's real renders,
each beside its target mockup. Host renders come from step 1's tool
(`tools/look_shots.py OUT --step N`); glass renders come from
`tools/board.py shot` on the T-Deck and P4 wherever the step changes them. The reviewer passes the step or lists every difference;
a difference stands only if it is RGB565 quantisation, real data, or a
deviation the commit names with its reason. A failed step goes back to its
worker, then to a fresh reviewer; a dependent step waits for the pass.

## The steps

### Engine: steps 1–4 run in parallel; 3 lands after 2

**1. The review kit.** Landed 2026-10-15. `tools/look_shots.py OUT
[SCENE ...] [--step N] [--theme NAME] [--variant MODE]` sets up each
`index.txt` scene on the host from the workstation the shell goldens build
(Sky Run's carousel, Star Catcher's code and frog, Calc keyed through the
target's tape, Calc over Files), renders it at the mockup's size and theme,
and writes `mockup | render` pairs, 3× for 320×240, with an `index.txt` the
reviewer reads. A scene is built when the shell holds its theme and every
step that targets it carries its Landed mark; until then it is the nearest
current screen (a missing theme drawn with `night`, Paper with `platinum`),
marked "not built yet" with what is missing. **Target** every PNG by name;
**net** `tests/test_look_shots.py`: every scene has a setup and plan steps,
and a few scenes render and pair; **goldens** none.

**2. RGB colours and roles** (GAPS 1). Roles take `#rrggbb`, quantised to
RGB565 into indices 64–127, which only the shell names; a cart's `pal()` and
screen palette never reach them. `PAL565` grows to 128 in every copy (host,
device, web, wasm, the board flush drivers). Every role keeps its nearest
MOY64 index for `theme()`, compiled apps' `theme` imports and cart canvases.
Other themes' previews and swatches are RGB565 bakes, as covers are, and take
no slots. §4.10's derivation and §7's check run on RGB, §7 holding every
non-text role to 3:1. New roles: `value`, `value_ink`, `lit`, `group_edge`,
`under`, `lcd`, `lcd_ink` (`sel` is `selection`). `format` and the seed
version are bumped, and a `moy` seed (dark and light) joins `night` with new
golden configs at both sizes, "the moy configs". **Target** the ramps of
`system_sheet.png`; **net** `tests/test_gate_pal_sync.py` at 128, the loader,
derive and readability tests on RGB, and a test that the live row, content
set and swatches fit 64 slots; **goldens** the Appearance picker; **perf**
`--store` and `--edit` on tdeck and p4, `tools/p4_conformance.py` on glass.

**3. Fonts** (GAPS 2). Galmuri7, 9, 11 (Bold, and regular for Contrast) and
GalmuriMono9 in the Moybyte cut, from a generalised
`tools/make_tiny5_font.py`, with a `THIRD_PARTY.md` notice; the byte map for
`− ← → ↑ ↓ …`; ellipsis on `moy_text_fit_px`; a `caption` role;
`MOY_FONT_SLOTS` to 6; the Code tab's cell from the mono advance. `moy` names
the fonts, so `night` keeps Tiny5. **Target** the type rows of
`system_sheet.png` and `moy-dark_handheld_code-editor.png` (57 columns);
**net** `tests/test_font_lane.py` and GAPS 2's Code-tab hit-tests; **goldens** the moy configs; **perf** `--repaint editor:code`
and `--store` on tdeck.

**4. 2× text and desk density** (GAPS 3, 7c). Files, Get Carts, the Storybook
and the editors move from 8 px cells to line metrics. Rows on the WM tier
are 14, and 17 in settings windows. Default window sizes are re-planned for
512×300. **Target** the Files window in `moy-dark_desktop.png`, and
`moy-dark_desktop_appearance.png`; **net** `tests/test_files_app.py` and the
editors' layout tests; **goldens** the handheld configs hold; the P4 windowed
configs re-baseline once; **perf** window drags on p4 and guition_p4.

### Parts: start after step 2; 5 then 6, with 7–10 in parallel

**5. Ring, bar and fill** (GAPS 7). The `focus`, `current` and `primary`
parts carry every kind's state, and nothing else does. The moon-glow halo is
Moy's only. **Target** `system_sheet.png` components, plus
`moy-dark_handheld_settings.png` and `moy-dark_handheld_calc.png`; **net**
`tests/test_ui_style_data.py` holds GAPS 7's rule for the three marks, and
every golden scene has exactly one ring; **goldens** every config, as one named re-baseline;
**perf** `settings scroll drag`, `settings tap row` and idle on tdeck and p4,
plus `--painter`.

**6. The new parts:** `keycap`, the `info` row, a `toggle` with its On/Off
word, `slider`, `seg` and the group caption. **Target** `system_sheet.png`
components; **net** a unit test per kind; **goldens** a parts-sheet config is
added; **perf** `--painter`.

**7. The `soft` frame style** (GAPS 4, 5). Paper and Studio are its
parameters. It brings a `lit` top edge, a `drop N` clause and Paper's
dithered drop. Minimise and maximise move to the chip and a title
double-tap. **Target** the windows in `moy-dark_desktop.png`,
`paper-light_desktop.png` and `studio-dark_desktop.png`; **net**
`tests/test_frame_styles.py`; **goldens** the moy windowed configs; **perf**
`window drag (settings)` and `desk idle, three windows` on p4 and guition_p4.

**8. The dim kernel** (GAPS 5, 6). One darken and blend kernel lands in the
canvas class with a `tools/p4_conformance.py` case. An unfocused window's
body is dimmed 25% once, on `win.buf`, at each focus change; dark themes
cache dimmed carousel covers, which the Player's fit check releases.
**Target** Files behind in `moy-dark_desktop.png`, and the neighbours in
`moy-dark_handheld_home.png`; **net** `tests/test_device_canvas_parity.py`;
**goldens** the moy configs; **perf** a new `focus switch` scenario on p4 and
guition_p4, which decides on GAPS 5's fallback. On tdeck, `--home` and `tools/board.py pmem`.

**9. Icons** (GAPS 8): the 12×12 two-tone set of 32 as a role sheet.
**Target** `system_sheet.png`'s icons; **net** `tests/test_icon_theme.py`;
**goldens** the moy configs.

**10. The star field** (GAPS 7b): a `stars` desk pattern laid into the
cached background. **Target** the moy PNGs' backgrounds; **net** a painter
test; **goldens** the moy configs; **perf** `--home` drag on tdeck.

### Screens: 11 first; 12 then 13, with 14–19 in parallel; 20 after 16 and 17

**11. Bar and tabs.** The bar holds the Moy button, then tabs or ‹ and a
title, then status. Desk chips use `raised` with a `value` underline, and
the bar band stays `bg` over any wallpaper. **Target** the bar of every PNG;
**net** `tests/test_top_bar.py` and `tests/test_bars.py`; **goldens** the bar
in every config; **perf** `--popovers` on tdeck and p4.

**12. The handheld home** (GAPS 9, 10). A 2× hero with 1× neighbours, the
title and metadata, a keycap hint strip and a 6-frame slide. Covers get 64
and 48 px crops (`CROPS`), and a cart without a cover gets a title tile.
**Target** `moy-dark_handheld_home.png` and `moy-light_handheld_home.png`;
**net** `tests/test_launcher_scroll.py` and
`tests/test_home_retained_frame.py`; **goldens** the tdeck and guition
configs; **perf** `--home` on tdeck and guition_s3. The `HOME_MOTION` and
`HOME_SCALE` verdicts go to #66.

**13. The desk home:** the hero; a details pane with source, last played and
play count (new store records, added rather than migrated) and Play, Change
and Copy; and a 64 px shelf that bleeds off the right edge. The bar's ×
leaves the home. **Target** the two `desktop_home` PNGs; **net** the
store-record tests; **goldens** `p4_1024x600_cs2_fs1_console`; **perf**
`--home` on p4 and guition_p4.

**14. Settings:** only rows that work, the About facts, and a caption on
every group. **Target** the two `handheld_settings` PNGs; **net**
`tests/test_settings_layer_pixels.py`; **goldens** Settings everywhere;
**perf** the settings scenarios on tdeck and p4.

**15. Appearance:** GAPS 11's Appearance row. **Target** the four `appearance` PNGs; **net**
`tests/test_appearance_app.py`; **goldens** Appearance everywhere; **perf**
`--edit` on tdeck and p4.

**16. Files:** Places (Projects, Carts, Art, Music and Trash), file
pictures, and Name, Kind and Size with ellipsis. Kind is dropped at large
type. **Target** Files in `moy-dark_desktop.png`. There is no handheld mockup,
so the review checks the handheld against DESIGN's rule; **net**
`tests/test_files_app.py`; **goldens** Files everywhere; **perf** `window
drag: files` on p4, and `--repaint files` on tdeck.

**17. Calc:** a tape history (new behaviour) on an LCD inset with the result
at ×3, and a 4×5 keypad with 2× legends. Focus is the ring and "=" is the
fill. **Target** the two `handheld_calc` PNGs and the Calc window; **net**
Calc's tests and a tape test; **goldens** Calc everywhere; **perf** `app idle:
calc` and `app drag: calc` on tdeck and p4.

**18. The code editor:** the editors' bar, the current line in `selection`
with the `value` bar, a 12 px overflow fade, and symbol keys in Galmuri11
Bold. **Target** the two `code-editor` PNGs; **net**
`tests/test_code_highlight.py` and `tests/test_code_editor_gaps.py`;
**goldens** the editor configs; **perf** `--repaint editor:code` and a Code
scroll on tdeck.

**19. The sprite editor:** a grey checker for transparency, with a slashed
swatch 0. The cursor is the ring around a black keyline. The current tool
and colour carry the bar. **Target** the two `sprite-editor` PNGs; **net**
`tests/test_sprite_reuse.py`; **goldens** the sprite tab; **perf** `editor
tab: sprites` on tdeck.

**20. The desk and windows:** an icon column of 26×20 tiles with labels on
`bg` plates, and the window states assembled whole. **Target**
`moy-dark_desktop.png` and `moy-light_desktop.png`; **net**
`tests/test_wm_windowed.py` and `tests/test_desktop_shell.py`; **goldens** the
P4 windowed configs; **perf** the default panel run on p4 and guition_p4.

### Wallpaper: starts after steps 12 and 20

**21. Wallpaper per glass** (GAPS 12). The wallpaper cart reads W and H,
receives the shell's quiet rectangles and declares a subject rectangle, which
the WM's default placement keeps clear of; `moybyte.moy_night` is redrawn in
MOY64 per glass without the wordmark. **Target** the two `wallpaper` PNGs;
**net** `tests/test_wallpaper_preview.py` and a placement test; **goldens**
the configs that show a wallpaper; **perf** `home idle, live wallpaper` and
`desk idle` paint zero on tdeck and p4.

### Themes: 22a, 22b and 22c in parallel; 23 after all three

**22a. Paper** (light only): dots, plates, inverted strips, a dithered drop
and undimmed neighbours. Windows behind cast no drop.

**22b. Studio** (dark only): square, flat, 1 px frames, white for current
and primary, and chrome labels in capitals through a theme flag on the
toolkit's label path.

**22c. Contrast**, refitted: Galmuri11 body, Galmuri9 captions, 15 px
keycaps, and a black keyline on the light ring.

For each, **target** that theme's PNGs; **net** readability for each variant, and the
picker for themes with one mode ("Light only" and "Dark only"); **goldens**
the theme's new configs; **perf** `--store` on tdeck and p4.

**23. The switch and the deletions** (GAPS 13). `moy` becomes the default;
`night`, `platinum`, `win95`, `machine`, `outline`, `indigo`, `berry`,
`forest` and `slate` are deleted, the last four returning as Moy accent and
tint presets with a dark and a light value per accent; the packages the
frame-style tests need move to `tests/fixtures/`; the seed version is bumped;
the step fixes its rows of the table below. **Target** every PNG; **goldens**
every config, one named re-baseline, the deleted seeds' configs retired;
**perf** the default panel run on tdeck and p4.

## The phase gate

- `tools/board.py pass` passes on tdeck, p4, guition_s3 and guition_p4.
- The Zero (`xiao_zero`) syncs a theme, and the browser console it serves
  draws in RGB.
- Every image is above its headroom floor.
- The default `tools/p4_bench.py` run on all four boards is recorded in #66
  and #58.
- A final context-free review passes every `index.txt` PNG against both host
  and glass renders.
